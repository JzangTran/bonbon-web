Drop one picture per pose here and the landing page uses it instead of the drawn stand-in (`../polar-bear.tsx`):

Rules for every picture:

- Transparent background (WebP or PNG), the whole bear in frame, feet at the bottom edge, about 1200 px tall.
- The same bear in every picture: same size, same apron, same light (soft studio light from the top left).

## Prompt (shared part)

> Photorealistic 3D render of an adult polar bear with true-to-life anatomy (long neck, small head, heavy shoulders, thick cream-white
> fur with fine strand detail), standing upright like a mascot, wearing a bright orange (#FE551B) kitchen apron with a small white heart
> badge on the chest. Soft studio lighting from the top left, gentle shadow under the feet, transparent background, full body,
> front three-quarter view, high detail, friendly but not cartoonish.

Then add the pose, for example for `serve.webp`:

> …holding out a white plate of steaming Vietnamese fried rice (golden rice, peas, diced carrot, egg) with both paws, mouth slightly
